Data deletion instructions

How to ask JLingo to delete information, and what there is to delete.

If you watch, follow, or comment on the JLingo Page

JLingo does not use Facebook Login and does not collect or store personal information about Page visitors, viewers, followers, or commenters. In most cases, JLingo holds no data about you to delete.

Your comments and reactions on the JLingo Page are stored by Facebook, not by JLingo. You can delete your own comments on Facebook at any time. For other Facebook data, see Meta's Privacy Policy.

How to ask for deletion

If you believe JLingo holds information about you, or a JLingo video or caption includes something about you that you want removed:

  1. Email jlingoeducation@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Describe the information, or give a link to the video or post it concerns.
  3. We will reply by email to confirm what we found and what we deleted. If we can't delete something, we will explain why.

What JLingo can delete

  • Lesson videos, captions, and publishing records stored on the JLingo operator's computer.
  • Videos posted on the JLingo Facebook Page, which the operator can remove from the Page.

JLingo keeps a record of each published video so it isn't posted twice by mistake. If a deletion request concerns a published video, we will remove the post and tell you what record, if any, we keep.

For the JLingo Page administrator

To stop the JLingo publishing tool from accessing the Page, remove the JLingo app from your Facebook account's Business Integrations settings. This revokes the access token the tool uses. The tool's local data can then be deleted from the operator's computer.
