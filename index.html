JLingo

Short Japanese vocabulary videos for Myanmar speakers.

JLingo publishes vocabulary lessons on the JLingo Facebook Page. Each video teaches ten Japanese words. Every word comes with its hiragana reading, its meaning in Myanmar, an example sentence with its reading, and a Myanmar translation. Lessons follow the JLPT levels from N5 to N1.

Every video is reviewed and approved by the JLingo operator before it is posted.

About the JLingo app on Facebook

JLingo uses a Meta (Facebook) app for one purpose: so the JLingo operator can publish approved lesson videos to the JLingo Facebook Page. The app is not offered to the public. It does not use Facebook Login and does not ask anyone else for access to their Facebook account.
