Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information JLingo accesses, stores, and shares, and how to ask for deletion.

Who we are

JLingo is run by the operator of the JLingo Facebook Page. You can reach us at jlingoeducation@gmail.com.

What this policy covers

  • The JLingo publishing tool. This is a Meta (Facebook) app and a private dashboard. Only the JLingo operator uses them, to publish approved lesson videos to the JLingo Facebook Page.
  • This website.

Your use of Facebook itself, including watching, reacting to, commenting on, or following the JLingo Page, is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy, not this one.

People who watch or follow the JLingo Page

JLingo does not use Facebook Login and does not ask Page visitors, viewers, followers, or commenters to connect their Facebook accounts. The JLingo publishing tool does not collect, store, or process personal information about them. It does not read comments, reactions, messages, follower lists, or Page insights.

What the publishing tool accesses from Meta

The JLingo Page administrator gives the tool access to the JLingo Page. It uses the Meta permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, and pages_manage_posts, and makes only these requests:

  • Publish a video to the JLingo Page, with the caption and optional title the operator approved.
  • Read the Page's ID and name to confirm the tool is connected to the right Page. This check posts nothing.
  • Read the link of a video the tool has just published.

What JLingo sends to Meta

JLingo sends Meta the lesson video file and the caption and title the operator approved. It also sends the access token that authorizes posting to the JLingo Page. These are lesson materials, not information about Facebook users.

What JLingo stores, and where

The publishing dashboard runs only on the JLingo operator's own computer. It is not publicly accessible. On that computer, JLingo stores:

  • lesson video files, captions, and titles;
  • approval and publishing records, such as when a video was approved and published, the Facebook video ID and link, and any error message returned by Facebook;
  • the Page access token and the dashboard passcode, in a private configuration file that is never published.

JLingo also has a Supabase database meant for lesson and curriculum data (vocabulary, lessons, and lesson scheduling). The publishing tool does not currently use it. It is not designed to hold, and JLingo does not put into it, information about Facebook users.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking, and no forms. The provider that hosts these pages may record technical information, such as IP addresses, in its server logs under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

JLingo does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers. Lesson videos and their captions are posted publicly on Facebook, which is their purpose.

How long we keep information

Lesson videos and records stay on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them. Publishing records are kept on purpose after a video is posted, so the same video is never posted twice by mistake. Videos posted on Facebook stay there until they are removed from the JLingo Page.

Deletion and questions

See the data deletion instructions, or contact jlingoeducation@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below.
